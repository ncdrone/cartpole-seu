Cart-Pole Fault Audit

Five independent adversarial reviews of SPEC-01, SPEC-02 and the LQR baseline. What breaks, how badly, and the order to fix it.

The soft-fault method (inject, classify, harden, re-measure, with a negative control) is the strongest part of the plan and most submissions will not have it. But the plan misses the first requirement twice: it cannot swing up from hanging-down, and the "optimization-based" answer is four constants from a Python script. On the protection side, the headline recovery does nothing in the shipped code, the force clamp is configured by the data it is meant to defend, and the campaign is built so it cannot fail. Every one of these is fixable inside the assignment budget, and the fixes make the story stronger than the one in the spec.

Cross-cutting themes · hit independently by two or more reviewers

Where the reviews agree

01

"Any non-moving start" is not met. Swing-up is required.

θ=π is a non-moving start and a stable equilibrium. No controller linearized about upright leaves it. Basin at 20 N / ±2.4 m is 44°; at 10 N, 35°; even unlimited force fails past 64°. Energy-shaping swing-up (Åström–Furuta) plus the existing LQR catch handles π in 2–3 s, but needs a deterministic kick at exactly π and hysteresis on the switch. The hardest start is near 85°, not π.

Critical01 · 04 · 05
02

The headline recovery is a no-op, and harmful in the PID variant.

Shipped defaults are KI_X = 0 and DFILT_ALPHA = 1, so "reset integrator and derivative memory" changes nothing observable. In a PID variant it is worse: an integrator reset leaves a permanent cart velocity of g·I_old (0.36–0.43 m/s, drives off-track) and zeroing derivative memory produces a 10–43 N kick against a 20 N clamp. Recovery must be a verified golden Params reload plus a bumpless filter reseed. LQR is nearly memoryless; persistent parameters are what ECC protects. That is a stronger radiation story, and it is true.

Critical01 · 02 · 03 · 04 · 05
03

The last line of defense lives in the data it defends.

force_limit and safe_force sit inside corruptible Params, and the fault branch emits safe_force unclamped. Flip the sign bit of force_limit and you get full-force positive feedback with no fault flag. Across all 256 single-bit flips of Params, exactly one produces a NaN/Inf; isfinite misses the rest. Every failing flip is a sign or exponent bit. Fix: clamp and fallback from compile-time constants applied after step(), plus a per-field range table independent of ECC.

Critical01 · 02 · 04
04

The campaign cannot fail. Verification is mislabelled as assessment.

Injecting into the encoded blob at rest, right before the check, only tests the codec. "Hardened shows fewer SDC than baseline" is true by construction. Faults inside the tick (locals, Input, the working copy) get written back and re-encoded as valid. Split into a Verification campaign (exhaustive, pass/fail, gates CI) and an Assessment campaign (all controller-owned state, same fault list per mode, N≈3,000 per mode for ±1 pt, Wilson intervals, never gated). Fixed 10 s runs hid 17% of real failures in the toy; use a fixed post-injection window against a fault-free twin.

Critical02 · 03 · 05
05

"Optimization-based" is currently four constants.

K comes from an offline scipy DARE solve; the binary performs no optimization. Solving the discrete Riccati equation in C++ at init is ~40 lines of 4×4 math with a scalar inverse and makes the claim literally true, gives a recompute-from-golden recovery path, and turns Q/R into the tuning story. Unconstrained "thin MPC" with a DARE terminal cost reproduces LQR exactly and a controls reviewer will know it. Receding-horizon iLQR or input-constrained MPC is the stretch, and its warm start becomes new SEU-sensitive state.

High01 · 05
06

Zero force is not a safe state for an inverted pendulum.

Open-loop pole at 3.97 rad/s: tilt e-folds every 0.25 s. Survivable outage at zero force is 0.5–1.3 s depending on angle; hold-last is strictly worse. A parameter flipped to Inf yields zero force forever. Define the fault response as "reload golden and resume within T ≤ ~300 ms", and put the freshness check on the actuator side in simulated ticks. An in-thread watchdog cannot see its own hang, and in a lockstep sim the hang scenario does not even exist unless the plant keeps integrating on the stale command.

High01 · 02 · 04 · 05
07

The compiler removes the protected blob (verified on this Mac).

Apple clang 17 at -O2 with injection compiled out kept a static blob in registers for 1,000,000 ticks, shrank three words to 1-byte bools, and wrote memory once after the loop. The check compares registers with registers. The ON and OFF builds are different programs. Fix: volatile word storage, decode once per tick into local Params/State, run the pure step(), encode back. Never -ffast-math (it folds isfinite to true); add #ifdef __FAST_MATH__ #error.

High02 · 03 · 04
08

Nothing protects the protector, and there is no fault management.

The mode word, fault counters, golden copy and FDIR state are all outside the protected region; one flip in the mode word silently downgrades SECDED to none. Output.fault is a per-tick flag that nothing counts, escalates, or acts on. Add a 4-state machine (NOMINAL → RECOVERING → DEGRADED → SAFE latched) with persistence counters, Hamming-distance-16 state codes, (v, ~v) counters, and a compile-time protection policy instead of a runtime mode word. NASA-HDBK-1002 and the cFS HS/LC apps are the precedent.

High02 · 04 · 05
09

The staged modes climb the wrong axis; pick one data scheme.

baseline → checksum → secded stages stronger codes on the same fault class, so stage 2 exists only to be beaten. Each stage should close a new class: none → data → data+compute → full. For the data scheme: CRC-32C (HD=6 at 416 bits, 2.9 ns with the intrinsic) over Fletcher-32 (HD=3), and Hsiao(39,32) on the F32 words using the OpenTitan encoder with an exhaustive codec test. Order matters: SECDED alone miscorrects 60–69% of triple-bit patterns, so decode first, then CRC over the corrected plaintext. Write corrected words back or "Corrected" counts inflate every tick.

High02 · 05
10

Docker is not reproducibility.

std::uniform_int_distribution is implementation-defined: libc++ (macOS) and libstdc++ (Linux) give different fault lists from the same seed. Clang defaults to -ffp-contract=on, so Apple Silicon fuses FMAs where x86 builds do not and borderline trajectories flip. Use an in-repo PRNG (splitmix64), generate an explicit faults.csv with a hash in the descriptor, build with -ffp-contract=off, and promise bit-exactness only on the same architecture.

Medium03 · 04
11

Scope is 16–24 hours against "a couple of focused hours".

Honest estimate of SPEC-01+02 as written. The failure mode is breadth without finish: a half-tuned controller, placeholder numbers, and slide 3 still reading "[fill after open decision]". Ship an 8–10 h MVP and say what was cut. Cut: Docker, gain-schedule LUT, CRC-vs-Fletcher debate, CFCSS, Reed-Solomon, MBU adjacency, async injection, bus CRC. Document, do not build.

The five reviews · internal notes, summarized here

Each reviewer's verdict

01 · Control theory11 findings

One spec failure, several stories that fall apart under a controls reviewer.

  • Basin is 44°, not "any". Swing-up plus LQR catch is 2–3 h.
  • ±10% sweep proves nothing: LQR survives all 27 corners easily. What breaks is the swing-up switch (+10% length failed from 30°) and Coulomb friction (4.5° hunting).
  • θ is never wrapped: θ=2π reads as a 360° error.
  • Success check hides SDC: a flip that zeroes K_x passed because only θ is checked. Require |x| → 0 too.
  • Never build with -ffast-math; never refactor clampf to std::min/max (NaN order-dependent).
internal review notes
02 · Memory protection18 findings

Protects what is easiest to demo, which is what hardware EDAC already covers.

  • Register promotion at -O2, verified. Volatile storage + decode-to-locals.
  • One blob for gains and state re-encodes corruption as valid. Params get a fixed reference CRC never recomputed; State re-encoded only after invariants pass.
  • Golden copy in RAM has an 8.5%/year chance of a latent flip at LEO rates if unscrubbed. Use params_default() from literals and pin its CRC in a test.
  • Sensor path bypasses every layer: add analytic redundancy (|Δθ − θ̇·dt| ≤ tol).
  • Includes a 6-step "if you only have 2 hours" order.
internal review notes
03 · Fault-injection method13 findings

A functional verification dressed as a reliability measurement.

  • Taxonomy is neither exclusive nor exhaustive. Log a mechanism column and a plant-outcome column; derive the five labels.
  • Half the candidate bits are dead (integ, thdot_filt, tick, pad). Show a field × bit-class heatmap, not one rate.
  • Negative test hits the wrong target: plant state is a physical disturbance, not a memory fault. Target the sensor snapshot, output, and CRC table.
  • N and CIs: showing <1% with zero failures needs n ≥ 299; distinguishing 8% vs 5% needs ~1,047 per arm.
  • Full campaign descriptor and result-row schema included.
internal review notes
04 · FSW architecture14 findings

Would not survive a real FSW review yet; the baseline is a good start.

  • No fault management: 4-state FDIR machine with table-driven transition tests.
  • Flight/sim boundary not drawn: Plant.cpp is linked into the fsw library and the injection flag changes the tested artifact. Split flight/ from sim/.
  • Bootstrap trap: command -v clang++ succeeds on a clean Mac without Command Line Tools.
  • F´ mapping: queued component in a rate group, PrmDb for golden params, throttled events, Health ping. FPP sketch included.
  • Ships a FSW-grade C++ constraint checklist; the baseline already ticks about half.
internal review notes
05 · Deliverable & framing14 findings

Misses the first requirement twice; the definition is a mechanism list.

  • Shipping PID with an explanation does not satisfy "optimization-based". Recommended: iLQR swing-up → LQR; energy swing-up as the fallback.
  • Rewrite the definition as a requirement with a coverage matrix; ~2 h of additions (dual-execute compare, output guard, plausibility, degradation ladder) widen it materially.
  • Results before disclaimers. Never say "radiation-hardened"; say "soft-error-tolerant under a stated fault model".
  • Includes the "10/10 answer", a risk register, a summary paragraph, and a requirements trace table.

Deduplicated across reviews

Open questions

  • The exact wording of "any non-moving start": if it includes hanging-down, swing-up is mandatory. The plan commits to swing-up.
  • Hour budget, and will it be stated in the README? (All reviewers: yes.)
  • Integrator at all? If not, delete ki_x/integ and rewrite recovery around golden reload. If yes, do LQI properly (5-state DARE).
  • SECDED for narrative, or TMR as the software-native scheme? If SECDED, cite ARGOS.
  • C++11 (CMake) or C++17 (SPEC-01)? F´ v4 requires C++14. Affects constexpr CRC and std::clamp.
  • Primary format: README + ≤5-min video, or slides? Pick one.
  • Double-bit in Params: reload within 300 ms, or declare DUE? Zero force forever is not an option.
Consensus

What the plan gets right

  • Inject → classify → harden → re-measure with the standard masked / SDC / DUE vocabulary.
  • A negative control on unprotected memory. This separates engineers from demo-builders.
  • Double-bit must never silently correct; never run on data you do not trust.
  • The compiler caveat in SPEC-02 §6.5 is the right instinct, just aimed at the wrong build.
  • Compile-gated, labelled injector; no Docker requirement; no fabricated Orca hardware claims.
  • The baseline code shape: pure step, POD blobs, explicit padding, bumpless filter reseed, simulated time only.
