These rows were measured on the first baseline, when the force limit and safe force still lived inside Params; they have since moved to compile-time constants, which is why the protected build cannot be given the first three flips at all. Of 256 possible single-bit flips in that Params, exactly one produced a non-finite value. The rest are finite and wrong, and the dangerous ones are all sign or exponent bits. Detection has to look at meaning (range) and integrity (codes), not at NaN.

3 · the same flips through the protected pipeline · computed

Where each flip is corrected, caught, or stopped

Now measured, not only computed: sim/campaign.py pushes every bit of every word through both builds. Unprotected: 42 of 640 single flips are silent corruption. Protected: 640 of 640 corrected, 60 of 60 double flips detected and recovered by golden reload, every check-bit and CRC-word flip handled, 13 compute-window flips caught by dual execution, every stall recovered. Three flips alias to a single-bit syndrome, so SECDED "corrects" the gain to −2.96 and reports success. The CRC over the corrected blob fails, and the range table rejects −2.96 for k2 independently. This is why the order is decode → CRC → range, and why CRC is not optional.

Miscorrection is not rare: over all 9,139 triple-bit patterns in a 39-bit word, extended Hamming miscorrects 69% and Hsiao 60%. Multi-bit upsets are more than 5% of space memory errors. The CRC exists for exactly this case.

4 · detect and correct vs detect and stop

The response ladder

Correction is only allowed when the code can prove which bit flipped. Everything else is treated as untrusted and replaced from a verified source. The plant sets the clock: at zero force the pole is unrecoverable after 0.3–1.3 s depending on angle, so every response below completes inside one 10 ms tick and the whole ladder must resolve within about 300 ms.

single-bit · SEC

Correct, scrub, count

Syndrome names the bit. Fix it in the local copy, write the corrected word back to the store, increment the correction counter. No command change, no state change. Telemetry only.

DED · CRC fail · out of range · u₁≠u₂

Reload golden, RECOVERING

Do not use the word. Rebuild Params from the compile-time literals, verify its CRC, re-encode the store, reseed the θ̇ filter from the current measurement (bumpless). Command this tick uses the reloaded gains. Exit to NOMINAL after M clean ticks.

second detection in window

DEGRADED

Entered on a second detection within the 200-tick window: a stuck bit, a corrupted golden, or a bug. Swing-up is inhibited (a swing-mode tick commands 0 N and flags the fault) and the clamp is halved to 10 N. Exits to NOMINAL after 200 clean ticks; any further detection latches SAFE.

still failing · or |θ| beyond limit

SAFE, latched

Force = 0, constant. Loop declared lost. Only an explicit command clears it. For this plant SAFE means the pole is down and the actuator is benign; there is no static command that keeps it up, so the ladder above is what "safe" really means.

Two things the ladder never does

  • It never "resets the integrator" as a recovery. With LQR the integrator is off; with PID a reset injects a permanent cart velocity of g·I and a derivative kick of up to 43 N. Persistent parameters are what get reloaded; dynamic state is reseeded from measurements.
  • It never holds the last command as a fallback. Hold-last is strictly worse than zero force at every angle for an inverted pendulum (survivable outage 330 ms vs 500 ms at 5°).

6 · try it

Reproduce the §2 rows

bash scripts/build_demo.sh                                   # build + verify
fsw/build/cartpole_demo --theta0 0.05 --seconds 6 --flip flim:31 --flip-tick 100 > flip.csv
python3 sim/render.py --csv flip.csv                        # watch the pole go over at 1.3 s
fsw/build/cartpole_demo --theta0 0.05 --seconds 6 --flip k2:30 --flip-tick 100 | python3 sim/render.py --csv /dev/stdin

Fields: k0 k1 k2 k3 ki ilim alpha flim safe, bits 0–31, IEEE-754 binary32 layout (bit 31 sign, 30–23 exponent, 22–0 mantissa). The injector lives in the sim-side main.cpp; the flight core has no knowledge of it. Built: on-board Riccati solve, θ wrap, energy swing-up, the encoded store with SECDED and CRC-32C, the range table, dual execution, the output guard from constants, and the 3-state FDIR with verified golden reload. §3 is now runnable: fsw/build/cartpole_demo --flip k2:30+31 --flip-tick 100. §3 becomes runnable once the store, decode and range stages land in the flight core (rows 1, 4 and 5).

Sources of the numbers: fsw/build/cartpole_demo --flip (§2); reference SECDED/CRC-32C codec in the session scratchpad, exhaustively checked (§3); research/01 survivable-outage table and research/02 miscorrection census (§4, §5).
