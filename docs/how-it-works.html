Cart-Pole Soft-Fault System

What one control tick does, what a flipped bit does to it, and where each flip is corrected, caught, or stopped. Every number below comes from this repo's binaries or from a codec probe, except one set of rows labelled historical and three figures from the literature.

The codec probe is not shipped: about 20 lines of C++ that link fsw/src/Protect.cpp and call secded_encode, secded_decode and crc32c on the golden Params reproduce every §3 value. The literature figures are extended Hamming's 69% triple miscorrection, the share of multi-bit upsets in space memory errors, and the CRC-32C Hamming distance.

The rest are finite and wrong, and all 42 that the campaign counts as silent corruption are sign or exponent bits (24–31). Detection has to look at meaning (range) and integrity (codes), not at NaN.

3 · the same flips through the protected pipeline · measured

Where each flip is corrected, caught, or stopped

sim/campaign.py pushes every bit of every word through both builds. Unprotected: 42 of 640 single flips are silent corruption. Protected: 640 of 640 corrected, 60 of 60 double flips detected and recovered by golden reload, every check-bit and CRC-word flip handled, 13 compute-window flips caught by dual execution, every stall recovered. Full table in the README.

The protected build stores each word with 7 Hsiao SECDED check bits and a CRC-32C over the 80-byte Params (golden CRC 0x9E919ECA, pinned at init). Check bytes, syndromes, SECDED verdicts and CRCs below come from a probe that links the shipped fsw/src/Protect.cpp (not shipped; about 20 lines reproduce it); the decision column is what fsw/build/cartpole_demo logs at tick 100 for the same flip (θ₀ = 0.05 rad).

The two triples. Three adjacent flips in k2 (0xC23D7EB4 → 0xC2DD7EB4) give syndrome 0x4A, which is the column of bit 24, so SECDED "corrects" bit 24 as well and reports success: four bits now differ and the gain reads −442.99. The CRC over the corrected plaintext fails, and cartpole_demo logs det 3 CRC at tick 100 (the miscorrection also bumps the SEC counter), reloads the golden and enters RECOVERING; the range table would have rejected −442.99 independently. For bits 0+1+21 the syndrome names bit 23 and the gain comes back as −78.75, inside k2's band, so the CRC is the only layer that catches it. That is why the order is decode → CRC → range, and why the CRC is not optional.

Miscorrection is not rare: of the 9,139 three-bit patterns of the 39-bit k2 codeword, the shipped Hsiao code reports SEC on 5,500 (60.2%; extended Hamming would be 69%). Of k2's 4,960 data-bit triples, 2,903 come back as a wrong word, and 1,062 of those are still inside the range band. Across all 20 words the probe finds 110,000 SEC-reported triples, every one a wrong word and every one caught by the CRC-32C, which has Hamming distance 6 at this 640-bit block (Koopman's CRC tables). Multi-bit upsets are more than 5% of space memory errors (literature figure).

4 · detect and correct vs detect and stop

The response ladder

Correction is only allowed when the code can prove which bit flipped. Everything else is treated as untrusted and replaced from a verified source. The plant sets the clock: measured with sim/campaign.py --deadline, a controller stall that begins at t = 1 s is recoverable for 0.6 s (from θ₀ 30°) to 1.3 s (from θ₀ 1°). Every response below completes inside the detecting 10 ms tick, well within the 300 ms budget.

single-bit · SEC

Correct, scrub, count

Syndrome names the bit. Fix it in the local copy, write the corrected word back to the store, increment the correction counter. No command change, no state change, and the tick still counts as clean. Telemetry only.

DED · CRC · range · u₁≠u₂ · non-finite · stall

Reload golden, RECOVERING

Do not use the word. Rebuild Params from the nominal model (defaults plus the on-board Riccati K) and accept it only if its CRC-32C equals the value pinned at init; re-encode the store; reseed the θ̇ filter from the next measurement (bumpless). After a decode-time or dual-execution detection the command this tick already uses the reloaded gains; after a non-finite output the tick keeps the fault path's 0 N and the reloaded gains take effect next tick. NOMINAL again after 50 clean ticks. An implausible sensor sample is first held without a reload; a second consecutive one reloads.

second detection while recovering

DEGRADED

Entered when a second detection arrives within the 200-tick window before RECOVERING has counted its 50 clean ticks: a stuck bit, a persistent input fault, or a bug. Swing-up is inhibited (a swing-mode tick commands 0 N and flags the fault) and the clamp is halved to 10 N. NOMINAL again after 200 clean ticks; any detection while DEGRADED latches SAFE.

3rd in window · any while degraded · bad golden · bad FDIR word

SAFE, no exit

Force = 0 N, constant, fault flag set, step() not run. Loop declared lost; only a restart clears it. For this plant SAFE means the pole is down and the actuator is benign; there is no static command that keeps it up, so the ladder above is what "safe" really means.

Two rules the ladder keeps

  • It never "resets the integrator" as a recovery. With LQR the integrator is off (KI_X = 0, and reseed zeroes it); with PID a reset injects a permanent cart velocity and a derivative kick. Persistent parameters are what get reloaded; dynamic state is reseeded from measurements.
  • It holds a stale command for 10 ticks at most. When the controller stops, the actuator monitor keeps the last command for STALE_MAX_TICKS = 10 (100 ms), then applies 0 N; when the controller resumes, the stall is a detection and the golden is reloaded. Measured from θ₀ 5°, a stall of up to 1.0 s is recovered.

6 · try it

Reproduce §2 and §3

bash scripts/build_demo.sh                                   # build + verify
fsw/build/cartpole_baseline --theta0 0.05 --seconds 6 --flip k2:31 --flip-tick 100 > flip.csv
python3 sim/render.py --csv flip.csv                        # the pole goes over at 2.1 s
fsw/build/cartpole_baseline --theta0 0.05 --seconds 6 --flip k2:30 --flip-tick 100 > flip30.csv   # rail at 4.91 s, falls at 5.19 s
fsw/build/cartpole_demo --theta0 0.05 --seconds 6 --flip k2:21+22+23 --flip-tick 100 | sed -n 102p   # tick 100: det 3 (CRC), fdir 1, sec 1, reload 1

Fields: k0 k1 k2 k3 ki ilim alpha swke swamax swamin swkx swkv sweref swenter swrate swexit mM mm ml mg, bits 0–31 (+ for several), IEEE-754 binary32 layout (bit 31 sign, 30–23 exponent, 22–0 mantissa). The injector lives in the sim-side main.cpp; the flight core has no knowledge of it. Built: on-board Riccati solve, θ wrap, energy swing-up, the encoded store with SECDED and CRC-32C, the range table, the input plausibility guard, dual execution, the output guard from constants, and the four-state FDIR (NOMINAL, RECOVERING, DEGRADED, SAFE) with verified golden reload. Every §3 row runs on cartpole_demo.

Sources of the numbers: fsw/build/cartpole_baseline --flip (§2 current rows); the first baseline build (§2 historical rows); a probe linking fsw/src/Protect.cpp (not shipped; about 20 lines reproduce it) and fsw/build/cartpole_demo --flip (§3); sim/campaign.py and --deadline (§3 note, §4); test_protect (triple census).
