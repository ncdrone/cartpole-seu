status · what exists vs what is drawn

Built today vs designed

Everything drawn in P2–P5 is built and runs in cartpole_demo. Only the flight/sim library split and typed frames of P1 are designed, not built. The measured numbers come from the binaries, sim/campaign.py and the back-tests; the codec values from a probe that links the shipped Protect.cpp (not shipped; about 20 lines reproduce it).
